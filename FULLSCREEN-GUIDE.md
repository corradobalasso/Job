# Viewing the Slideshow in Fullscreen

Quick instructions for viewing this page in fullscreen, browser by browser.

## Chrome (Windows / macOS / Linux)

- Press **F11** to toggle fullscreen on and off.
- macOS alternative: click the green **full-screen button** in the top-left
  corner of the window, or press **⌘ + Ctrl + F**.
- To exit, press **F11** again (or **Esc**, or move the mouse to the top of
  the screen and click the fullscreen icon that appears).

## Firefox (Windows / macOS / Linux)

- Press **F11** to toggle fullscreen on and off.
- macOS alternative: click the green button in the top-left of the window,
  or press **⌘ + Ctrl + F**.
- Or open the menu (**☰** in the top-right) and choose **Full Screen**.
- To exit, press **F11** again (or **Esc**).

## Microsoft Edge (Windows / macOS)

Edge is Chromium-based, so it behaves the same as Chrome:

- Press **F11** to toggle fullscreen on and off.
- To exit, press **F11** again (or **Esc**).

## Opera (Windows / macOS / Linux)

Opera is also Chromium-based:

- Press **F11** to toggle fullscreen on and off.
- Or open the Opera menu (top-left "O" icon) and look for the fullscreen
  option.
- To exit, press **F11** again (or **Esc**).

## Safari on iOS (iPhone / iPad)

iOS Safari has no F11-style fullscreen toggle — the address bar and toolbar
will always reappear when you tap or scroll. Two ways to get a cleaner,
closer-to-fullscreen view:

**Option A — Add to Home Screen (recommended for repeated use)**
1. Open the page in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** and confirm.
4. From now on, open the page via its icon on the Home Screen instead of
   through Safari — it launches without Safari's address bar or tab bar.

**Option B — Guided Access (true single-app lock, no way to swipe away)**
1. Go to **Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access** and turn it on.
2. Open the page in Safari (or via the Home Screen icon from Option A).
3. Triple-click the **side button** (or **Home button** on older iPhones/iPads).
4. Tap **Start** in the top-right corner.
5. To exit later: triple-click the side/Home button again and enter the
   Guided Access passcode (set the first time you turn it on).

Guided Access is the closest iOS equivalent to a real kiosk mode — it hides
all system UI and prevents leaving the page until unlocked.
